About JingBear

JingBear is a commercial equipment brand of Fullness of Life (Shanghai) International Trade Co., Ltd. We help international buyers source equipment and related items through a coordinated supplier network.

We connect the product, the supplier and the purchase plan.

As a trading company, we organize sourcing, product coordination and export preparation around each buyer’s request.
